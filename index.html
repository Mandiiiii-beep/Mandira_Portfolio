Team Project · First Year
Delivero — Autonomous Delivery Robot

A robot built to ferry documents and stationery between faculty offices, cutting out manual trips. Runs on a dual-microcontroller design — an Arduino Mega handling real-time navigation and motor control, an ESP32 serving a Wi-Fi web app for destination selection and live status.

The hard part: a digital compass and gyroscope pair keep headings stable through turns, and ultrasonic sensors handle obstacle detection — getting it to drive straight through several burned-out boards was the real engineering lesson.
